## Prompt library (copy into Google Flow)

### V03 — **CREATE THIS FIRST** (WhatsApp bill)

10-second cinematic ad, Indian kirana counter, merchant saves bill on **Bolo Bill web portal** (laptop browser, Create Bill screen). **Share on WhatsApp** modal, customer phone shows WhatsApp opening **bill link** (phone is secondary; hero = browser). Slow Hinglish male VO: *"Bill browser se banao—customer ko WhatsApp par link, paper bill ki tension kam."* Text overlay max 6 words: **"Bill link on WhatsApp"**. End card **Bolo Bill** only. No app store, no URL. 16:9, warm light.

### V02 — **CREATE SECOND** (Create Bill)

10s, full-screen **web browser**: Bolo Bill **Create Bill**—scroll catalog, add to cart, total updates, save. Shopkeeper at counter on laptop. Slow Hinglish VO: *"Counter par bill turant—item choose, cart mein total, web portal se ready."* Overlay: **"Fast bill on web"**. End **Bolo Bill**. Not a phone app UI.

### V01 — **CREATE THIRD** (Brand / paper → portal)

10s, split: messy paper register vs **Bolo Bill merchant portal** in browser (dashboard glimpse). Slow Hinglish VO: *"Dukaan ka bill aur stock ab browser mein—Bolo Bill. Paper se digital, ek hi jagah."* Overlay: **"Bills & stock—online"**. End **Bolo Bill**. Laptop/tablet, no URL.

### V06 — Dashboard & sales

10s, **Dashboard/Sales** in browser: today/week tiles, chart glimpse. VO: *"Aaj ki bikri ek nazar—Bolo Bill dashboard, browser se hisaab clear."* Overlay: **"Sales dashboard"**. **Bolo Bill** end.

### V09 — Stock live

10s, **Stock** page: categories, quantities, low-stock feel. VO: *"Stock browser mein live—kam padne par turant pata, counter smooth."* Overlay: **"Live stock on web"**. **Bolo Bill** end.

### V04 — Menu import

10s, **Menu Import** wizard: upload menu photo/list, preview items, confirm. VO: *"Purani menu? Web par upload—items stock mein jaldi."* Overlay: **"Import menu on web"**. **Bolo Bill** end.

### V10 — Hide revenue + PIN

10s, top bar **Hide revenue** → masked totals; staff taps show → **PIN modal**; owner PIN → visible. VO: *"Staff portal use kare—revenue chhupao; dikhane ke liye aapka login PIN."* Overlay: **"PIN to show totals"**. **Bolo Bill** end.

### V05 — Invoices

10s, **Invoices** table, search, date filter, open bill. VO: *"Saare bills ek list—search aur date se purana bill turant."* Overlay: **"All bills in one place"**. **Bolo Bill** end.

### V07 — Items sold

10s, **Items Sold** table/chart. VO: *"Kya zyada bikta hai—report web par, decision easy."* Overlay: **"What sells best"**. **Bolo Bill** end.

### V08 — Out of stock

10s, **Out of Stock** list on web. VO: *"Jo khatam—OOS list browser mein, team ko turant pata."* Overlay: **"Out of stock list"**. **Bolo Bill** end.

### V11 — QR

10s, **QR Code** page in portal, print at counter. VO: *"Dukaan ka QR—web portal se set, customer scan karke jud sake."* Overlay: **"Your store QR"**. **Bolo Bill** end.

### V12 — Language & settings

10s, **Settings** tiles, language switcher. VO: *"Panel apni language mein—Bolo Bill web settings."* Overlay: **"Your language"**. **Bolo Bill** end.
